A curated view of ongoing ALS/FTD research—from molecular mechanisms and human disease biology to biomarkers, therapeutic development, and clinical translation.

Scope / Coverage

A connected view of the disease landscape

Six lenses keep the Radar broad enough to follow the field while retaining a clear path from biology to patient impact.

01

Spectrum & phenotypes

Motor, cognitive, behavioural, and language phenotypes across the ALS–FTD continuum, including the sources and consequences of clinical heterogeneity.

Watch: Disease definitions, longitudinal progression, and patient-relevant outcomes.
02

Genetics & molecular mechanisms

C9ORF72, SOD1, TARDBP, FUS, GRN, MAPT, and convergent biology involving TDP-43, RNA metabolism, proteostasis, autophagy, mitochondria, and neuroimmune signalling.

Watch: Mechanistic convergence, cell-type specificity, and causal evidence.
03

Biomarkers & patient stratification

Genetic, fluid, imaging, and digital measures—including neurofilament light—for diagnosis, prognosis, target engagement, and treatment response.

Watch: Validation, assay performance, and usefulness within defined patient groups.
04

Models & human data

iPSC systems, organoids, animal models, post-mortem tissue, and single-cell, spatial, and multi-omic studies that connect experimental mechanisms to human disease.

Watch: Reproducibility, model validity, and translation across biological scales.
05

Therapeutic pipeline

Antisense, RNA and gene therapies, small molecules, immunomodulation, and strategies targeting protein homeostasis, autophagy, and other shared disease pathways.

Watch: Target engagement, biological rationale, safety, and clinical-stage evidence.
06

Trials, regulation & patient priorities

Trial endpoints, platform and adaptive designs, recruitment, regulatory decisions, and how patient experience can shape meaningful measures of benefit.

Watch: Design choices that account for heterogeneity and accelerate interpretable results.
