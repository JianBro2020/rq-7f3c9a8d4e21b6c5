02 / Research

Questions, methods, and the evidence they produce.

My research connects mechanistic molecular and cell biology with computational analyses of disease-relevant human data, with a particular focus on autophagy, mitochondrial quality control, innate immune signalling, and ALS/FTD.

01 / Topics

Research programmes

Each topic will become a concise case study: the question, your contribution, the result, and why it matters.

01

How does the ALS/FTD-linked C9orf72 complex engage the autophagy-initiation machinery?

Connecting the C9orf72 complex to autophagy

I identified and characterised a direct, phosphorylation-regulated interaction between SMCR8 and FIP200. My work defines a molecular link to autophagy initiation and shows selective effects on Parkin-dependent and deferiprone-induced mitophagy.

Protein interactionsAutophagyMitophagyFlow cytometry
02

Can the STING-TBK1 interaction be selectively perturbed to modulate a pathway implicated in neuroinflammation?

Modulating TBK1-dependent cGAS-STING signalling

I demonstrated that peptide fusion to TBK1 disrupts cGAS-STING signalling and characterised an unreported mechanism regulating the STING-TBK1 interaction. I also identified a protein interaction connecting this pathway to fatty-acid synthesis.

Innate immunityNeuroinflammationTBK1Lipid metabolism
03

How does ULK1/2-dependent phosphorylation tune interactions between FIP200 and selective-autophagy cargo receptors?

Phosphoregulation in selective autophagy

I characterised FIP200 phosphorylation by ULK1/2 and how it differentially regulates cargo-receptor association, contributing to a second-author manuscript now being finalised.

Selective autophagyPhosphorylationFIP200Biochemistry
04

How do disease-associated genes and pathways vary across motor-cortex cell types and ALS subgroups?

C9ORF72-ALS motor-cortex transcriptomics

I independently conceived and built an end-to-end analysis of single-nucleus RNA-seq data from 26 donors, from raw FASTQ processing through cell annotation, donor-level pseudobulk differential expression, and pathway enrichment.

snRNA-seqHuman ALSPythonGSEA
